# Cinematic 2D-HD art direction

Remaster-only presentation. MAME supplies original game logic and audio, never the displayed framebuffer.

Use the 1994 film actors' likenesses, costumes and realistic proportions. Preserve the actual arcade performers where the arcade casting differs from the film. Verify each performer before producing final assets.

Initial references: Guile (Jean-Claude Van Damme), Ryu (Byron Mann), Ken (Damian Chapa), Chun-Li (Ming-Na Wen), Cammy (Kylie Minogue), Bison (Raúl Juliá). Film references are not proof of arcade casting.

Redraw every animation pose with consistent face, costume, silhouette, anchor and timing. Use transparent RGBA textures, cinematic lighting and independently reconstructed backgrounds. No anime, cartoon redesign, native sprite upscale or original graphics mode.

Acceptance: verify likeness, pose alignment, animation continuity, transparency, gameplay synchronization and asset rights before distribution.
